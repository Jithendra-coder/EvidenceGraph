Financial research begins with a question. EvidenceGraph brings indexed SEC filing passages and annual SEC Company Facts into one workspace. Inspect each passage in its filing, trace reported facts to their source and period, and compare annual values with the calculation visible. Keep the question, evidence, and caveats together in an exportable research note. Search uses lexical matching, and any AI-assisted draft needs human review. EvidenceGraph supports research. BUILT FOR TRANSPARENCY

Trust begins with clear boundaries.

EvidenceGraph keeps source references visible and describes what each result represents. Image-only PDFs and complex visual tables need separate review. It is a research workspace, not an investment recommendation.
